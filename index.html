Pencil, fountain pen, felt pen, marker, watercolour, blend, diagram, fill, eraser, cut and move, text
Cut and move (0 or X)
Loop part of the drawing, then drag it. Drag the corner square to resize and the round knob to rotate (hold Shift to snap), or use the wheel and Shift+wheel. D duplicates, S saves it to Clips, Delete removes, Enter sets it down.
Better cut-outs
Your loop is smoothed for you. Just tap an object with the scissors to select it. Snap to object tightens a rough loop to the object's edge, and Grow / Shrink widen or tighten the selection.
Clips (L)
Your saved cut-outs. Tap one to place it on any page.
Text (K)
Tap to type, tap text to edit it. Tab or a quick sideways swipe switches Title, Heading, Body and Bullets. On storyboard paper, text snaps to the caption lines under a panel.
